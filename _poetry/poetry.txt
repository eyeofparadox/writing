NINEVEH : BODY AND SOUL

A vision poured into an open eye
To touch the soul within
And inner eye awakes to fashion
What, humble mind and will
Would recognize--its own daughter

A feeling like fright and sin
Was all she could realize
"Is that me?" she asks
The sound, a voice of wonder
"It always has been," is her own reply

Passion is the perfect promise
Stripped of fear and apprehension
The flesh itself would part
Give up to such appetites that
Reason itself would falter

Do I dread, so much my sound seeming?
Not more for what I might attain  
This anguish a realization
I can no more part
Disorganize and member

As risk the unknown embraced
That I would lie at your hand
"Not so," she chides and smiles
"We have always found our way,
We can again should we wake together."

A promise being true and passion 
Being pure, and deep and wild
I trust, allow the flesh its hope
Open to such elusive paradox
And it is itself so enigmatic able

I ache and honor almost
The idea as felt a phantom
That senses aspire but can not commit
A transcendence of form
And in my harrowed heart, I try

O gods, but on this will of mine
I would touch the purest depth
And in my own manner become
As one to you as I am wont
"And as one, we are"

PART I : STORIES



I wait
In the aching of my rage
	The second turns
Within the sensual flesh
In the ghost of its depths
Embraced within
The idea
	Unrealized
Tense
	Expectant

I wait
In this carnal frame--my cage
	The spirit yearns
Again, if the flesh is willing
Shall the sense transcend the flesh
A silent promise
A fear
	Unrealized
Virtual
	Profound

I wait
The spirit seething--rage
	And desire burns
Consuming the will of flesh
To defy the shattered mind
Tempting oblivion
Even tempting
	Life
To know
	To feel

	Why do I wait?

PART II : LIES

I turn to words as if
This quiet desecration 
Of emptiness might sate
The empty ache 
Within
I cannot answer

To turn a phrase and so 
Express my hesitation
To lie upon my fate
But lie awake
Again
I do not wonder.

PART III : LANGUAGE

It's a little bit like falling
Spinning great concentric fright
I feel it always calling
Looming fiercly in the night

This hollow vision fills me
My breath it always takes
I wonder that it kills me
My death it always shakes

It--vaulting--arcs across the sky
It flows, a part of everything
It--pulling--sparks a vivid find
A truth--pure, stating everything

What awareness could ever ride
Safely in this maelstrom
And hope to reach the other side
As whole as what once made from

It's a little bit like falling
Spinning great concentric fright
I tremble with its dawning
Looming fiercly in the night

This hollow vision fills me
My breath it always takes
I shudder like it thrills me
My death it always shakes

It arcs, vaulting across my mind
It flows, a part of everything
It pulls, sparking a vivid find
A truth--pure, stating everything

What awareness could ever ride
Safely in this maelstrom
And hope to reach the other side
As whole as what once made from?

I am not actually prone to seizures. The inspiration for this poem was the result of an allergic reaction I had during a business trip. The seizure struck in the middle of the night and I lay wide-awake and helpless as my body shook uncontrollably. The experience was oddly graphic in detail, burning itself into my mind so deeply that I relived the ordeal frequently in dreams. My inability to really describe the experience, compounded by dreams, eventually led to the creation of this poem.

How near at hand it is

PART V : TEMPTATION

Light falls
And strikes my soul
Death calls
And leaves me cold
I see
The great Abyss
Could be
A thing like this
Can I
Take back control?
Am I
Not still whole?
What mystery
Is this thing
That history
Did never sing?
The answer
Is in my grasp
One chance or
One final gasp?
I can
Not lose tonight
In this stand
For my first sight!

PART VI : SECRETS

I come unto the line
The part that is all things
Wedged between the unimaginable

I search for some sign

Swallowed by the indiscribible
Flowing into immensity
I grasp the true meaning

But the words are just holes

Splinters of insanity
And I find myself screaming
As shards of reason close

And I bleed with my soul

Meaning comes from confinement
Reality comes from restriction
Life comes from death

Arranged in a continuity

Awareness escapes through abandonment
Completeness arrives through perception
Self, lost to transcend death

And there is no sanity

PART VII : THRESHOLDS

How strange, when I woke--my eyes
The world poised at my finger tips
So wonderous and so profound to realize
How things led to this

No matter that I'd found the meaning
All was made meaningless
So blind, we always stumble, seeking
So painful, bare, a truth like this

No emotion and no salvation
This great mystique--electric cold
As pure as thought, this whole solution
Has no heart, eternal, old

As a truth, without dispute
What has 'ere been
Mere mortals could not dare refute
such absolute precision

EPILOGUE

I am the dreamer and I am the dream
Alone and my own and yet never seen
I am within and I am without
I am the one who coupled with doubt
I am the slayer and I am the slain
Taking my pleasure embracing the pain
A passionate phoenix torn

I am the artist and I am the art
With patience and anguish I figured it out
I am the voice and I am the tone
I am the vision my dreaming has shown
I am the will and I am the way
And I am the coin with which I must pay
A choice I'll never mourn

I am the actor and I am the act
body and vessel that bloods the pact
I am the master and I am the slave
I am the soul I've struggled to save
I am the promise that is calling
Entering freely, awakened and willing
To lose the mask I've worn

I am the pattern and I am the part
I am the focus, the stop and the start
As I am the brilliance, the burning star
I am also the darkness that stretches afar
By all that is sacred, profound and profane 
The moment has come to claim my domain
I am my own daughter born

Ah, I know you, dragon, coiled so and deep
To think I found you sleeping near; I think--
	I have often wondered
	Though less often more than now--
How I could recognize
And yet, how could I not
As, oh so well I know you, so well I know you not?

Well, bide awhile, dragon--hear, although you sleep
Pray, let me whisper in your ear; I speak:
	I found what you wanted
	So, shall I tell you how?
Now--do you realize, 
That this was both our lot
As, bound we are to searching, we both have what we sought?

How so, my fine dragon? please, your patience keep
It soothes my heart to find you here; my link
	while wit, perhaps, is squandered
	On your wise and noble brow
Thou I could tantelize
Have tantelized--I've wrought
As, born of flame from ashes, siezed that for which we fought.

Do you know me dragon?  Does recognition creep?
Feel how I embrace you; feel I have no fear.  You drank
	I was not plundered
	I rose and sought you out
Proud, your phoenix flies

As, tried, I learned, writhed, I burned, and you have left me not.
 


Cherish this now, for
It was not easily got
Our road was hard, and

But we have not lost the lot

Embrace what you have opened
Drink that succulent naught
