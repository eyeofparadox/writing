
who wants to live 
when love must die
when all you give
becomes a lie
my blood and bone
my weary eyes
bereft and lone
beset by sighs
undone by love
and dreams gone by
the time has come
to say goodbye



These days come
And there is no explaining
Never worse than some
From them there's no escaping
Not where I come from
And not where tears are raining

When the mind goes numb
In the face of suffering
Of depths I cannot plumb
Or speak of without stuttering
I cannot play dumb
When I could be comforting

Whatever has become
There's ever more becoming
Where pain and ache now thrum
May love and joy come strumming
And when this dark day's done
A new day will be dawning

Drawn into the heart of everything
Where between the unimaginable
And all that is humanity
I come upon the line

Conditioned to confine the meaning
To these restrictions we assemble
Life and death into reality
I search for some sign

A path to parse the truth in anything
Swallowed by the indiscribible
Flowing into immensity
I can not define

The words mere holes devoid of meaning
As shards of reason crumble
Into splinters of insanity
I see the design



I come unto the line
Found drawn at the heart of everything
Wedged between the unimaginable
And all that is humanity

I search for some sign
A path to parse the truth in anything
Swallowed by the indiscribible
Flowing into immensity

But the words are just holes
And I find myself screaming
As shards of reason crumble
Into splinters of insanity



Meaning comes from confinement
Reality comes from restriction
Life comes from death

Arranged in a continuity

Awareness escapes through abandonment
Completeness arrives through perception
Self, lost to transcend death

And there is no sanity
