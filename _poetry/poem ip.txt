so, the world is this
the spirit subsumed in flesh
cause unto effect
laid low unto death
and in doubt
it is lived
and for profit
we are enslaved
savage
in its civilized form
relentless
in its endless demand
unsympathetic to our cries
of lament
it defines itself as knowledge
feeding on our breath
delighting in our sighs
reducing us to beggars
pleading for it to endow us
though it blinds us
to receive its gifts
