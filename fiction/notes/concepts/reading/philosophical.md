## **philosophical**

* **Understanding life's complexities:** It suggests an awareness of the inevitable ups and downs of life and a capacity to cope with them without excessive emotional distress. \[[5](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/philosophical)\]

* **Seeking deeper meaning:** Philosophical thinking involves questioning assumptions, exploring underlying principles, and seeking a deeper understanding of reality. \[[6](https://www.quora.com/What-does-it-mean-to-be-philosophical-And-why-is-it-a-good-thing-to-be), [12](https://www.nagwa.com/en/explainers/473164169802/#:~:text=Philosophical%20thinking%20involves%20pursuing%20knowledge%20and%20searching,the%20values%20that%20a%20particular%20society%20holds.)\]
