Unravelled

Classic rock played softly in the background as my tired mind turned and turned around the notion of sleep. I longed for it. I knew I had so little time for it. It was taunting, cruel; only coming when so many other things needed doing, sleep danced beyond my grasp. In its wake shifted the ashes of so many thoughts, dreams, hopes and frustrations. I wonder how I came to this. I try to wrap my mind around something worthy of the space my words are channeled toward. A class. An obligation, slipping, drifting through my hands like fine, dry sand. 

My eyes burn, dry tears cracking as I blink and try to focus. When was it simple? When was it easy to break things down into entities and attributes? It was back when those words had different meanings for me and spoke of living things, not atoms of fact. Fact. Facts. The cold dry ache of inches upon inches of snow that drinks every drop of moisture from the air, leaving my lips and throat parched. I lie atop my blanket, too tired to chase down inspiration, rising again and again to chase away my thirst. Useless. In between sips, the dry, bitter taste of smoke curls upon my tongue. 

I imagine it sounds depressing, but I am indifferent. This wasteland is so far beyond depression. I am tempted to sigh, but I stop at a deep breath and close my eyes. There is patience deeper than my frustration. There is dedication in spite of this loss of coherence in thought and purpose. Not tonight. Not a good night. I am too drunk on confusion. I had a thought, an urge to participate, but thinking is more like dreaming at the moment. I should sleep. I really need to sleep.
