**Conceptual Treatments Across Domains**

**Science & Psychology**
- **Psi (Ψ)**: Symbol of psychology, derived from *psyche* (Greek for soul/mind).
- **Mainstream View**: Psi phenomena are largely considered untestable or falsified by empirical methods.
- **Quantum & Information Theory**: Some fringe theories link psi to quantum entanglement or non-local information fields.

**Mysticism & Parapsychology**
- **Spiritualism**: Psi powers seen as gifts from the spirit world.
- **Parapsychology**: Attempts scientific validation of psi (e.g., Ganzfeld experiments, remote viewing).
- **Transpersonal Psychology**: Psi as a gateway to expanded consciousness and human potential.

**Fiction & Pop Culture**
- **Fantasy**: Psi powers often overlap with magic (e.g., mind control, telepathy).
- **Science Fiction**: Psi framed as mutation, evolution, or advanced tech (e.g., *Dune*, *X-Men*, *Foundation*).
- **Superhero Media**: Psi abilities like telekinesis and mind-reading are staples (e.g., Jean Grey, Professor X).
